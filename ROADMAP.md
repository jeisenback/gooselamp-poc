# Drift - Roadmap

The slice in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-12-metroidvania-slice-design.md` is three deliverables, each working on its own.

- **M1 - Foundation.** Repository, harness, world state, compartments, switches, vents, the full movement kit, the swing against switches and hittables, sound. `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-12-metroidvania-slice-1-foundation.md`
- **M2 - Combat.** Health and damage, the fire projectile, hit stop, the three enemies, the Warden, airlock checkpoints, enemy respawn. `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-12-metroidvania-slice-2-combat.md`
- **M3 - The slice.** The thirteen rooms, `WorldGraph` and the reachability test, room loading, the map, the exit, the playtest runner, the web export. `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-12-metroidvania-slice-3-the-slice.md`
